SHE QUIT LIFTING FOR T100 TRAINING — HERE'S WHAT SHE LOST

A 220 Triathlon writer stopped chasing squat and deadlift PBs and gave three months to swim, bike and run for her first T100.

She kept some lifting in, but cut the volume and the loads hard.

When she went back to the barbell, the numbers had dropped — but not as far as she expected, and her endurance had climbed.

That is the tradeoff nobody warns you about when you sign up for a long-course race: something in your training week has to give.

Most age-groupers pretend it doesn't, then wonder why they're injured by April.

The honest version is you pick two things to be good at this season, and you accept the third one slides.

What did you drop from your week to make triathlon training fit?

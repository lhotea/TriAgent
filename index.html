She dropped the weights for three months. Here's what happened.

220 Triathlon writer India Paine spent months chasing strength PBs in the gym before switching fully to swim, bike and run to prep for her first T100.

After three months of reduced lifting, she went back to the barbell to measure the damage.

The short version: the weights got heavier, fast.

Endurance volume eats into maximal strength within weeks, and the deeper you go into a race build the harder it is to hold on to what you had.

The usual fix is one or two short, heavy sessions a week through race prep — not hypertrophy work, just enough load to protect the neural side.

Most age-groupers drop lifting entirely once the plan gets hard, then wonder why they get injured in month four.

How many strength sessions do you actually keep in your race build?

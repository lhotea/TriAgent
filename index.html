She quit lifting for T100 training. Here's what her strength did.

220 Triathlon writer India Paine spent months chasing personal bests in the squat rack before switching entirely to swim, bike and run for her first T100.

Strength sessions dropped to almost nothing for three months while the training load moved to endurance.

When she went back under the bar, the numbers told her exactly what triathlon had cost her.

This is the tradeoff every age-grouper hits: two gym sessions a week feel non-negotiable until the long ride, the brick and the swim squad all want the same hours.

Most of us quietly drop the weights first and hope the damage is small.

Her experiment is worth reading before you make the same call for 2026.
